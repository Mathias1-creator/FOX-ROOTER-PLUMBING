The Story

Meet Dave Fox.

Fox Rooter & Plumbing is led by Dave Fox, a second-generation plumber with over 12 years in the trade. Plumbing runs in the family — Dave grew up around the work and learned it the right way, from the ground up.

He started Fox Rooter & Plumbing to give LA homeowners something harder to find than it should be: a plumber who answers the phone, shows up, explains the problem in plain language, and fixes it for good — not with a patch that fails a month later.

From a simple clogged drain to a full water-line replacement or gas line repair, every job gets the same standard: honest pricing, clean work, and respect for your home. And because plumbing doesn’t wait for business hours, Dave is available 24/7.
